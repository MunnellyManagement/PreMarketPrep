Auction Market Audit

Pre-Market
Structure Audit

A five-step checklist for reading overnight auction context before the RTH open.

Completion
0/5
Q1
Overnight Session
Where did overnight seek value?

Did Globex explore above, below, or stay inside prior RTH value area?

Q2
Overnight Close
Did overnight close confirm the direction?

A close in the direction of the probe = commitment. Close back inside = probe only.

Q3
Price Location
Is price currently testing the prior session extreme?

Both setups require price at a key level — if not there yet, wait for it to come.

Q4
Rejection at Extreme
Was there a clear rejection at the extreme?

Decisive buyer/seller response at the level = failed auction signal. Accepted through = continuation signal.

Q5
Value Area Location
Is price inside or outside prior RTH value area?

Outside value = directional auction in play. Inside value = balanced, mean reversion favoured.
